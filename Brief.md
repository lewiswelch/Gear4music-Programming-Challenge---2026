# Brief

Part 1: Gear4music is growing into Europe. The InventoryInterface and ProductIndexer need to be updated to support multiple languages, currencies, and per-country-visibility rules.

Your task is to demonstrate how this can be done. You've been given up to 1 hour to put something together.

Showcase your best practice knowledge of PHP.

You will need to reorganise the PHP files, while keeping the codebase neat and well structured.

Part 2: Only if you have time or wish to further demonstrate your skills and experience.

Gear4music now wants to offer digital products, as well as products in varying conditions (e.g. new, used, refurbished, perhaps others).

Show us how your solution could be altered to meet these requirements.





Additional Guidance:

Please provide a solution that best demonstrates your ability using one of two equally valid approaches; both will be assessed solely on the quality of the resulting solution:

a. Create a bespoke PHP response independently, drawing on your own knowledge and experience. State in your accompanying documentation that you chose this approach and explain why.

b. Create a bespoke solution influenced, informed, or guided by third-party tools. In your accompanying documentation, explicitly state which tools you used, where and how you used them, and why.

We do not wish to put you under pressure with this assessment. Feel free to detail your approach in documentation, especially for part 2, if you are running low on time.
